Can someone who has never built a dashboard make one with the current editor?
We ran moderated usability tests with eight people who use a smart home but
aren't the main Home Assistant admin in their household, and who had never
created a dashboard. Participants shared their screens and talked through what
they were doing as they built a living room dashboard, added cards for lights,
air conditioning, a window cover and a media player, set a heading and
rearranged the layout.

We looked at whether new users can finish a dashboard, where they get stuck and
why, and where their expectations clash with the interface. The full method and
findings are in the research wiki.
