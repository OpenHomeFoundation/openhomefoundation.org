We ran moderated usability tests with eight people who use a
smart home but aren't the main Home Assistant admin in their household, and
who had never created a dashboard.

Participants shared their screens and talked through what they were doing as
they built a living room dashboard: adding cards for lights, air conditioning,
a window cover and a media player, setting a heading, and rearranging the
layout. We asked whether new users can finish a dashboard, where they get stuck
and why, and where their expectations clash with the interface.

The findings are ranked by how many people hit each problem and how much it
affected them, and each comes with an opportunity for improving the editor.
The full analysis is in the research wiki.
