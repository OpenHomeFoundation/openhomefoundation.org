“What’s new in 2026?” section

[Update copy]

Community Day has been running for two years, and we try to do better each time. Here’s what we changed for the  2026 edition:

Open door

We invited communities from all of the foundation’s major projects – alongside the Home Assistant community, there were ESPHome and Music Assistant-specific meetups for the first time!

More support

We rolled out a dedicated Community Meetups Playbook, with clearer reimbursement guidelines and an official merchandise request form, to help hosts run their events more smoothly.

A new look

We refreshed the event’s palette to represent the whole foundation, with the colors we use for one of our three core principles: sustainability – because it’s our community who sustains us 💚
